Battery and Electrical System Issues

Common Problems:
- Dead battery
- Weak battery
- Alternator failure
- Loose connections

Symptoms:
- Car not starting
- Clicking sound when turning key
- Dim headlights
- Battery warning light ON

Possible Causes:
- Old battery (3-5 years lifespan)
- Corroded battery terminals
- Faulty alternator
- Parasitic drain

Safety Advice:
- Avoid repeated jump starts
- Turn off lights when engine is off

Recommended Actions:
- Check battery voltage
- Clean battery terminals
- Replace battery if needed
- Test alternator output
