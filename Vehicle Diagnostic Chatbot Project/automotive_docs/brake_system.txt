Brake System Diagnostics

Common Brake Issues:
- Squeaking brakes
- Grinding noise
- Soft brake pedal
- Brake failure

Causes:
- Worn brake pads
- Brake fluid leak
- Air in brake lines
- Damaged brake discs

Symptoms:
- Reduced braking efficiency
- Vibrations when braking
- Noise while braking

Safety Advice:
- Do not drive if brakes are not working properly
- Maintain safe stopping distance

Recommended Actions:
- Replace brake pads
- Check brake fluid level
- Inspect brake discs and calipers
- Get professional inspection
