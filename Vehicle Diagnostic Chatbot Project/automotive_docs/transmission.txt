Transmission System Issues

Common Problems:
- Gear slipping
- Hard shifting
- Delayed gear engagement
- Transmission overheating

Causes:
- Low transmission fluid
- Worn clutch plates
- Faulty torque converter
- Internal wear

Symptoms:
- Jerking motion
- Delay in acceleration
- Burning smell

Safety Advice:
- Avoid driving with slipping gears
- Do not ignore transmission warning signs

Recommended Actions:
- Check transmission fluid level
- Replace transmission fluid
- Inspect gearbox
- Visit mechanic immediately
