Engine Issues and Diagnostics

Common Engine Problems:
- Engine knocking
- Engine overheating
- Engine misfire
- Loss of power

Causes of Engine Knocking:
- Low octane fuel
- Carbon buildup
- Incorrect ignition timing
- Worn connecting rod bearings

Engine Overheating Causes:
- Low coolant level
- Faulty radiator
- Broken cooling fan
- Thermostat failure

Symptoms:
- High temperature gauge
- Steam from engine
- Burning smell
- Reduced performance

Low Oil Pressure Symptoms:
- Oil warning light ON
- Engine ticking noise
- Engine overheating

Safety Advice:
- Stop driving immediately if overheating
- Do not open radiator cap when hot
- Check oil and coolant levels

Recommended Actions:
- Check engine oil level
- Refill coolant
- Inspect radiator and hoses
- Visit mechanic if issue persists
